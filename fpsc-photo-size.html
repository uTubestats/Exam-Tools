FPSC Photo Size 2026: What the Form Actually Accepts

Updated February 2026. Always confirm the exact size in the official FPSC notice for your specific post — requirements can change.

Short answer

FPSC application forms typically ask for a recent photograph in JPG format, usually under a small KB limit (commonly 20 KB to 50 KB), and a signature scan under a similar limit. The most common rejection is not the photo itself but its file size — applicants upload a 2 MB phone photo and the portal refuses it.

Why the KB limit matters more than the pixels

Most candidates spend time worrying about the picture's dimensions. In practice, the portal usually accepts a reasonable pixel size but is strict about the byte size. If your file is 800 KB and the form allows 50 KB, it will fail regardless of how good the photo looks. The fix is compression, not retaking the photo.

The three things that get photos rejected

1. The file is too large

This is the most common. A modern phone camera produces 2–5 MB per photo. You need to bring it under the limit before uploading. Use the resize to KB tool, enter the limit from your form, and download the result.

2. The format is wrong

If you took a screenshot or saved an image from WhatsApp, it may be PNG or WebP. Most Pakistani exam portals only accept JPG. Convert first with the PNG to JPG tool, then resize.

3. The photo is not recent or not front-facing

FPSC requires a recent, front-facing photo with a plain background. A cropped group photo or a selfie with a busy background can be rejected at the verification stage even if the file size is correct.

Step-by-step: fix a rejected photo on your phone

  1. Open the FPSC form and note the exact KB limit stated.
  2. Take or select a recent front-facing photo with a plain background.
  3. Open the resize tool, enter the KB limit, and select the photo.
  4. Click Resize Image and download the result.
  5. Upload the downloaded file to the form.

What about the signature?

The signature scan has the same problem: it is usually a phone photo that is far too large. Sign on white paper with a black pen, photograph it in good light, then resize it to the KB limit the form states. A signature that is too dark or too small can be rejected — keep it clear and centred.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading the original camera photo without compressing it first.
  • Using a screenshot of a photo instead of the actual file.
  • Choosing the wrong file format (PNG instead of JPG).
  • Not checking the official notice — some posts have different limits.

Quick checklist before you upload

  • File format is JPG
  • File size is under the stated KB limit
  • Photo is recent, front-facing, plain background
  • Signature is clear and under its own KB limit

If a photo still fails after this, the issue is usually the format or the photo content, not the size. Check both before retrying.

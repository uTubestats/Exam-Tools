Convert PNG to JPG

Most Pakistani exam portals accept only JPG. If you have a PNG, WebP, or a screenshot, convert it here in one click. Your file is processed on your device and never uploaded.

Select a PNG or WebP image or drag it here

When to convert to JPG

  • Your form says "only JPG/JPEG allowed"
  • You uploaded a screenshot and the form rejected it
  • Your file is a PNG and too large — JPG is much smaller
  • You downloaded an image from WhatsApp or a website as WebP

After converting

JPG files are usually much smaller than PNG, but if your form has a strict KB limit, run the converted file through the Resize to KB tool.

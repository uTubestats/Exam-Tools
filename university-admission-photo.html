University Admission Photo Size in Pakistan (2026)

Updated February 2026. Requirements differ by university — always check the official admission portal notice.

Why university portals reject photos

Most public university admission portals in Punjab, Sindh, and KPK accept a JPG photo under a small KB limit. Applicants typically upload a phone photo of 2–5 MB and the portal refuses it. The fix is compression, not a new photo.

What to check before uploading

  • Format — usually JPG only
  • File size — usually a small KB limit (commonly 20–100 KB; check your portal)
  • Pixel size — some portals also specify width × height
  • Photo content — recent, front-facing, plain background

How to fix a rejected photo

  1. Note the exact KB and pixel limits from the portal.
  2. If the format is wrong, convert with the PNG to JPG tool.
  3. If a KB limit is stated, use the resize to KB tool.
  4. If pixel dimensions are stated, use the pixel resize tool.
  5. Upload the downloaded file.

Portals commonly used in Pakistan

Universities in Punjab, Sindh, and KPK often use their own admission portals, and some use a shared central system. The exact size is stated on the upload page of each portal. Do not assume the size from another university applies to yours.

If the form is not clear

If the portal does not state a KB limit, choose a file around 50–100 KB in JPG format. That size is accepted by most Pakistani admission portals and stays sharp enough for verification.

Common mistakes

  • Using a photo from another application that was cropped for a different portal.
  • Uploading a screenshot instead of the actual image.
  • Not checking the pixel-size requirement when one is stated.

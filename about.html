About This Site

This site exists for one reason: Pakistani exam and job portals reject photos because of file size, and most free tools online are cluttered, slow, or upload your photo to a server.

Every tool here runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Everything is free and requires no account.

I am a solo developer based in Pakistan. I built these tools after dealing with form-upload errors myself while applying for exams and university admissions. If a tool here does not work for your form, email me and I will look at it.

I am not affiliated with FPSC, PPSC, NTS, or any university. Sizes stated in the guides are common examples — always confirm the exact size in your official form notice.

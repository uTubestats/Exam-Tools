Common pixel sizes used in Pakistan

  • 413 × 531 — passport photo (35×45 mm at 300 DPI)
  • 200 × 230 — many university admission portals
  • 600 × 600 — profile photos and some job portals
  • 300 × 300 — small avatar / signature crops

Always confirm the exact numbers in your official form notice. These are common examples, not guarantees.

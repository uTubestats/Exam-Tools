How to use this for Pakistani exam forms

  1. Find the exact size your form states (for example, "50 KB" or "20 KB").
  2. Type that number in the box above.
  3. Choose your photo and click Resize Image.
  4. Download the result and upload it to the exam portal.

Common questions

Why does my image not reach the exact size?

Very detailed photos need more bytes to stay sharp. If the result is slightly under your limit, that is correct — forms usually accept "under the limit," not "exactly the limit."

Will this work for FPSC, PPSC, or NTS?

Yes, as long as you enter the size the form asks for. Check the official notice for the exact KB and pixel size — it can change year to year.

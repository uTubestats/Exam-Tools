What makes a valid passport photo

  • Front-facing, eyes open, neutral expression
  • Plain light background (white or off-white)
  • Recent — usually within 6 months
  • No cap or heavy shadow on the face
  • Head and top of shoulders visible

Size details

35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI equals 413 × 531 pixels. This is the standard used across most Pakistani government and university portals. If your form asks for a different KB limit, run the result through the Resize to KB tool afterwards.

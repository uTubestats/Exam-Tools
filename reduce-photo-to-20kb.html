How to Reduce a Photo to 20KB on Your Phone

Updated February 2026.

Quick method

  1. Open the resize to KB tool on your phone.
  2. Type 20 in the size box.
  3. Tap Choose Photo and pick your image.
  4. Tap Resize Image.
  5. Download the result and upload it to your form.

The whole process takes under a minute and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Why 20KB is a common limit

Many Pakistani exam and job portals set a small limit to keep their own storage and bandwidth low. 20KB is small but usually enough for a clear passport-style photo if the image is not too detailed. Photos with plain backgrounds compress better than busy ones.

What if the photo looks blurry after resizing?

20KB is a tight limit. A very detailed photo may lose sharpness to fit. Two ways to improve the result:

  • Use a photo with a plain background.
  • Crop tighter around the face before resizing.

If the form allows a higher limit (say 50KB), always use the higher limit — the photo will stay sharper.

What if the tool cannot reach 20KB?

Some images, especially ones with fine detail or noise, cannot compress below a certain size without becoming unreadable. In that case, crop the image first with a photo editor, then retry. Very rarely, a photo cannot reach a limit — choose a simpler, clearer photo.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading the original file and assuming it was resized.
  • Downloading as PNG (PNG is much larger than JPG).
  • Not checking whether the form also asks for specific pixel dimensions.
